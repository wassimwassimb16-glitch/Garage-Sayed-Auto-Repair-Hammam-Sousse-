# Hair Style By Oussama – Image Assets

This directory contains visual assets for the website.

### Guidelines for Shop Owner / Admin
1. **Never hotlink Google Maps images** directly due to terms of service and CORS limitations.
2. Replace `shop-1.jpg` through `shop-6.jpg` with high-resolution photos of Oussama's barber shop in Sousse medina (exterior, interior, haircut in action, close shave, tools, medina street).
3. Recommended resolution: 1200x800px or 1600x1060px, compressed WebP or JPG (<250KB each).
4. `hero-poster.jpg` is the fallback poster displayed while the 3D barber pole loads or on low-power devices.
5. `og.jpg` is the 1200x630px social media preview card for Facebook, WhatsApp, and X/Twitter.
